Tigera announces Lynx to address governance challenges with autonomous, non-deterministic AI agents that traditional security tools, built for deterministic workloads, cannot handle. The platform consolidates five capabilities: discovery via an agent registry, posture management with AI-CSPM and sandboxing, cryptographic identity, unified policy enforcement at the gateway, and anomalous behavior detection. Lynx aims to close the gap between AI teams needing experimentation velocity, platform teams needing provable control, and security teams ensuring accountability.
